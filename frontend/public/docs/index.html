PPBudget Overview

A privacy-focused, multi-tenant personal budget tracker with automated bank syncing and smart categorization.

🎯 Purpose

PPBudget is a personal budget tracker designed from the ground up for multi-user isolation. Whether you are managing your personal finances, organizing household expenses with family members, or hosting the application for multiple independent users, PPBudget ensures complete data segregation and privacy.

Every registered user receives their own isolated environment. Your financial accounts, balances, transaction logs, category schemes, and automation rules are strictly scoped to your account and never shared or exposed to others.

✨ Core Features

PPBudget combines essential personal finance tools with powerful automation to make tracking your money effortless:

🛡️

Multi-Tenant Isolation

Strict user separation guarantees complete privacy for accounts, transactions, and custom settings across all users.

🔄

Automated SimpleFin Importing

Connect your financial institutions via SimpleFin Bridge to automatically sync bank accounts and recent transactions.

⚡

Rule-Based Categorization

Define flexible rules to automatically categorize transactions, clean merchant descriptions, and streamline imports.

📊

Flexible Budgeting

Establish category-level budgets, monitor your spending limits in real-time, and stay on top of your financial targets.

📅

Subscription Tracking

Identify and manage recurring subscriptions, track billing intervals, and avoid unexpected renewal charges.

🔍

Transaction Review Workflow

Review incoming transactions, mark them verified, and maintain accurate financial records with ease.

⚠️ Caveats & Considerations

💡
SimpleFin Account Required for Automated Sync

To use the automated bank synchronization features, an active SimpleFin Bridge account is required. SimpleFin serves as the secure conduit connecting your banking institutions to PPBudget. If you do not use SimpleFin, transactions can still be managed manually within the application.

💻 System Requirements

PPBudget is built with modern web technologies and has minimal client-side requirements:

  • ✓ Any Modern Web Browser: Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, Microsoft Edge, or Chromium-based browsers.
  • ✓ Responsive Cross-Platform Support: Accessible from desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and smartphones.
  • ✓ JavaScript Enabled: Required for interactive dashboard features, real-time charts, and data management.
