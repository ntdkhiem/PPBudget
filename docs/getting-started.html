Getting Started with PPBudget

Follow this beginner-friendly, step-by-step onboarding tutorial to set up your account, configure categories, build your monthly budget, organize accounts, and track recurring subscriptions.

Step 1

Setting up a New Account

To begin tracking your finances with PPBudget, create your personal user account.

How to Register:

  1. Open PPBudget in your web browser and navigate to the Register page (or click Create an Account from the login screen).
  2. Enter your email address in the Email Address field (e.g., you@example.com).
  3. Enter a secure password in the Password field. (Must be at least 6 characters long).
  4. Re-type your password in the Confirm Password field to prevent typos.
  5. Click the Create Account button.
💡
Automatic Login: Upon successfully registering, PPBudget generates your authentication session token and directs you immediately to your personal Dashboard.
Step 2

Creating Categories

Categories allow you to group transactions into meaningful financial buckets (e.g., Groceries, Dining Out, Rent, Salary). Categories form the backbone for your budgets, reporting, and automated rules.

Category Types Explained:

  • Expense: Money flowing out for goods, services, and living costs (e.g., Groceries, Utilities, Entertainment).
  • Income: Inbound revenue and earnings (e.g., Salary, Investments, Freelance).
  • Transfer: Movement of funds between your own accounts that does not alter your net worth (e.g., Credit Card Payment, Savings Transfer).

How to Add a Category:

  1. Click Categories in the left sidebar (or go to Settings → Categories).
  2. In the creation bar at the top, type your desired name into the New Category Name input (e.g., Groceries).
  3. In the dropdown menu, select the category type: Expense, Income, or Transfer.
  4. Click the + Add button. Your new category will instantly appear under its corresponding section below.

Managing Existing Categories:

  • Edit Category: Hover over any category row and click the Pencil icon. Edit the name or category type inline, then click the green Checkmark icon to save changes.
  • Delete Category: Click the Trash icon on a category row. If transactions are currently assigned to that category, PPBudget will display a warning dialog confirming how many transactions will become uncategorized before deletion.
Step 3

Setting up Budgets

Budgets let you allocate target spending limits for each category during a specific calendar month and track your real-time pacing.

How to Create a Monthly Budget:

  1. Click Budgets in the sidebar navigation.
  2. Use the Month Selector (with < and > arrows) at the top to select the target month (e.g., August 2026).
  3. Click the + New Budget button in the upper right corner.
  4. In the modal dialog:
    • Choose your target category from the Category dropdown (e.g., Dining Out).
    • Enter the maximum allowed spending in the Monthly Limit ($) field (e.g., 450).
  5. Click Save Budget.

Understanding Budget Visuals & Pacing:

  • Total Month Overview: Shows aggregate monthly spend across all budgeted categories combined. A vertical Today (Day X) pacing indicator compares your current calendar progress with your spending velocity.
  • Status Indicators: Displays On track for the month if spending is under the daily expected pace, or Pacing ahead of schedule if spending is running faster than expected.
  • Avg / Day & Safe / Day: Each individual budget card calculates your average daily expenditure so far, plus the exact Safe / Day dollar amount you can spend for the remainder of the month without exceeding your limit.
  • Quick Inline Limit Editing: Click the Pencil icon on any card to update its monthly limit without opening a separate form.
Step 4

Creating Accounts

Accounts represent where your money lives (bank accounts, cash, investments) or what you owe (credit cards, loans). PPBudget organizes accounts into Assets and Liabilities to calculate your net financial position.

Account Types in PPBudget:

  • Asset: Bank checking accounts, high-yield savings accounts, physical cash, and brokerage portfolios.
  • Liability: Credit cards, student loans, auto loans, mortgages, and personal debt.
  • Income / Expense: Dedicated ledger accounts used for tracking specialized revenue or outflow streams.

How to Add an Account Manually:

  1. Click Accounts in the sidebar.
  2. Click the + Add Account button in the top right.
  3. In the dialog form, enter:
    • Account Name: A recognizable label (e.g., Main Checking or Sapphire Credit Card).
    • Account Type: Select Asset or Liability.
    • Initial Balance ($): The starting balance on your statement (e.g., 2500.00 for cash/checking, or current balance owed on a credit card).
  4. Click Create Account.
ℹ️
Preparing for Automated Bank Sync: If you plan to link your real bank accounts with SimpleFin, you can create your accounts here first or have them automatically configured when syncing. Read the SimpleFin Importer Guide for full details.
Step 5

Tracking Subscriptions

Subscriptions keep recurring charges (streaming services, gym memberships, software licenses, domain renewals) organized so you never get surprised by auto-renewals.

How to Add a Subscription:

  1. Click Subscriptions in the sidebar.
  2. Click the + Add Subscription button.
  3. Enter the subscription details:
    • Name: Service name (e.g., Netflix, Spotify, Cloud Storage).
    • Amount ($): The recurring cost (e.g., 15.49).
    • Billing Cycle: Select Monthly or Yearly.
    • Next Billing Date: Choose the next scheduled payment date.
  4. Click Save Subscription.

Linking Subscriptions to Transactions:

When you record a manual transaction or review imported transactions:

  1. Open the Add Transaction or Edit Transaction modal.
  2. In the Subscription dropdown, select the matching subscription name.
  3. Once linked, PPBudget automatically marks the subscription as Paid This Period with a direct link to the transaction date.
  4. If a billing date arrives and no matching transaction is found, the dashboard displays an alert such as Expected today or Overdue.

Subscription Summary Cards:

  • Active and expected subscriptions: Total dollar value of all active subscriptions.
  • Expected and not yet paid: Remaining subscription charges expected before the period ends.
  • Expected monthly costs: Normalized monthly cost (automatically dividing yearly subscriptions by 12 for accurate monthly budgeting).
